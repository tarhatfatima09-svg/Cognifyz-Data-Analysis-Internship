# Cognifyz Technologies – Data Analysis Internship
## Level 2 – Task 3: Geographic Analysis

### 1. Objective
To analyze the geographical distribution of restaurants using latitude and longitude coordinates, identify approximate areas with high restaurant concentrations, and visualize the results.

### 2. Dataset
- Original restaurant records: 9,551
- Records excluded due to missing or placeholder coordinates: 497
- Records used for geographic analysis: 9,054
- Geographic attributes: Latitude and Longitude

### 3. Methodology
1. Loaded the restaurant dataset using Pandas.
2. Identified records with missing coordinates or coordinates equal to (0, 0).
3. Excluded these records from geographic analysis while preserving the original dataset.
4. Saved the cleaned geographic data.
5. Rounded latitude and longitude to two decimal places to group restaurants into approximate geographic areas.
6. Counted restaurants in each area and sorted the results in descending order.
7. Generated visualizations showing geographic distribution and the ten most populated approximate areas.

### 4. Results

The analysis used 9,054 restaurant records after excluding 497 records with missing or placeholder coordinates.

### 5. Key Insights
- A total of 9,054 records were included in the geographic analysis.
- The most populated approximate coordinate area was latitude 28.63 and longitude 77.22, containing 147 restaurant records.
- All ten leading approximate areas fall within a relatively concentrated region around New Delhi.
- Excluding the (0, 0) coordinates removed a misleading concentration that could distort geographic visualizations.
- Grouping coordinates after rounding to two decimal places provides an approximate comparison of restaurant concentrations; it does not represent formal clustering or prove exact neighborhood boundaries.
